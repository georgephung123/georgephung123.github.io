# Pre-Charlotte Funsies!

My family came down to my apartment at my university to travel to Charlotte! We decided it would be best to come during the weekend after my sister's spring break as they can come on Friday, sleep at my place, then commute to Charlotte in the morning.

My mother and I actually received an Instacart order (an app where you get paid for delivering groceries) at around 9PM on Friday, being offered an 18 dollar tip! That did mean that we had to immediately get out from the comforts of my apartment to driving out to a groecery store within a 3 minute span. Unfortunately, we did not realize that delivery included *three* full packs of water bottles, and the delivery destination invovled a flight of stars and a decent amount of walking around the apartment complex. Hey, at least it paid for the electricity for the trip!

We planned to make a stop first at Banner Elk, as it has a scenic alpine coaster that seemed pretty fun to try.
